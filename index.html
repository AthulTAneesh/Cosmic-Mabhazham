OPPY

Opportunity was designed to study the geology of Mars and look for signs of ancient water.

MISSION PROFILE

Launched
07.07.2003
Planned life
90 sols
Actual life
5,111 sols

WHAT OPPY ACHIEVED

A rover that kept on rolling.

  • Found evidence that liquid water once shaped the Martian surface.
  • Drove about 45 km, farther than a marathon, on another world.
  • Reached and explored the rim of Endeavour Crater.

WHAT OPPY FOUND

Evidence of a Mars that once held water.

Rocks, soil and craters carried clues to environments that were once wet.

A JOURNEY THAT EXCEEDED EXPECTATIONS

90 days
became 15 years.

JUNE 2018

When the Sun went silent.

A planet-wide dust storm blocked Opportunity’s solar panels. NASA declared the mission complete on 13 February 2019.
